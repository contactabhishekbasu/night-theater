The framing

Two models, one rule

Neither model is ever the judge. Verdicts are decided by comparing our reduction to the published truth — the models only triage attention.

post-reduction Grader — an advisory watchdog

After a night is reduced, the grader estimates p(good): is this fit clean enough to be worth a human's time? The deterministic rules stay authoritative; the ML grader is a challenger that retrains daily and is only ever a second opinion.

pre-observation Probing — a shadow forecaster

Before we observe, the probing model forecasts whether a night is worth attempting. It is shadow-only and in evaluation: it logs predictions and steers nothing until it clears a strict promotion gate and a human signs off.

The one rule: a reduction is called science-grade only when our fitted transit lands within 2σ of the published values — a comparison to the literature, never to a model. No model output ever appears in a science result.
